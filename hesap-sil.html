Lian — Hesap ve veri silme

Uygulama: Lian (Google Play: com.balicik.lian) · Geliştirici: balicik · English below

1. Uygulama içinden (hemen)

  1. Lian'ı açın ve Ayarlar'a gidin.
  2. Hesap bölümünde Hesabı sil'e dokunun.
  3. Onay penceresinde silmeyi onaylayın.

Silme anında ve kalıcıdır.

2. Uygulama olmadan (e-postayla)

Uygulamaya erişiminiz yoksa furkanbali885@gmail.com adresine, mümkünse giriş yaptığınız Google e-posta adresinden "Lian hesap silme" konulu bir e-posta gönderin. Hesabınız ve sunucudaki bütün kopyalarınız en geç 30 gün içinde silinir ve size bildirilir.

3. Ne silinir, ne kalır

Lian — Delete your account and data

App: Lian (Google Play: com.balicik.lian) · Developer: balicik

In the app (immediate): open Lian → Settings → Account → Delete account, then confirm. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Without the app (by email): email furkanbali885@gmail.com with the subject "Lian account deletion", preferably from the Google email you signed in with. Your account and all server copies are deleted within 30 days and you will be notified.

Deleted: your server notebook (learning progress, notes, custom words, study journal, preferences); your account record (user ID and, with Google sign-in, email, name and profile photo link); any earlier non-Google backup, on request by email; the session on your phone.

Kept: learning data on your phone is deliberately kept until you uninstall the app. Handwriting samples you chose to send are kept because they are not linked to your name, account or device and cannot be identified as yours; samples not yet sent are deleted from your phone with Settings → Handwriting data → Send my drawings → Off.

To delete only part of your data without deleting the account, email the same address. Details: privacy policy.
